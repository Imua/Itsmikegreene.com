WHY I STARTED

The challenge is about consistency.

Last year, two friends and I challenged ourselves to move three miles a day for 30 days. We each took on the challenge in our own way, and it showed me how much consistency can change what feels possible. Progress isn't always about one extraordinary workout. Sometimes it's about showing up again tomorrow.

That period of consistent running helped me reach 177 pounds and build toward nearly 12 miles at a comfortable 8-minute-per-mile pace. This year, I want to invite more people to take on their own version of that commitment.

Move-ember isn't a race. It's a reason to move, every day.

THE GROUND RULES

Show up your way.

  • The Original 90: Run or run/walk at least three miles on each of the 30 days.
  • Move Every Day: Walk, run, or combine both for three miles each day.
  • Treadmill miles count. There is no minimum pace.
  • Each day's three miles must be completed that day. Extra miles cannot replace a missed day.
  • If you miss a day, stay with us. Every day you do complete still matters.
  • Listen to your body. If you're new to daily exercise, consider walking or a shorter personal goal rather than pushing through pain or injury.

QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does it cost anything?

No. MOVE-EMBER 2026 is free to participate in.

Do I need Garmin or Strava?

No. Use any activity tracker, a treadmill, or a simple daily distance record. You can also join our optional Strava Club for community and encouragement.

Can I walk?

Yes. Choose the Move Every Day category if you want to walk, run, or combine the two.

What if I miss a day?

You can keep participating. A perfect 30-day streak is a specific achievement, but missing a day doesn't mean you should stop moving.

Will there be T-shirts?

Maybe! Optional merchandise may be offered later. Buying anything will never be required to participate.
